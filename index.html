How It Works

Up and running in under 2 minutes

No technical knowledge needed. Just plug in your phone and click start.

1

Install DataBridge PRO

Download the ZIP, extract, double-click install.py. Python and ADB are set up automatically.

2

On your phone: Settings → Developer Options → USB Debugging ON. Connect via USB cable.

3

Open MEmu Emulator

Launch MEmu and wait for Android to fully boot. DataBridge detects it automatically.
